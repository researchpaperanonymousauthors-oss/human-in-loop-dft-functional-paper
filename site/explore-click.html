Click-to-reveal energy map explorer

Replay mode. The Skala energies were computed in advance and are stored. Each click reveals one of them. The cheap xTB map is corrected as you reveal points, and the barrier estimate updates after every point.

How to read the map

Horizontal: the direction of the moving proton as seen from the centre of the frame, 0 to 180 degrees (the other half is the mirror image). Vertical: its distance from the centre in angstrom. Colour: energy above the lowest point, in kcal/mol; dark is stable. White circles are points you revealed; orange squares are suggestions. This is a map of where the proton sits, not a reaction path, so the barrier is the lowest pass between two wells on this map.

Suggested next points

Suggestions come from simple rules: where the estimated path is highest, wells not yet measured, where the estimate is least certain, and the lowest estimate left. You can ignore them.
