Auto-play demo: a simulated clicker

Simulated clicker: a fixed rule, not a person. It replays 20 clicks on the stored Skala energies. The page recomputes the map and the barrier estimate after every click with the same code as the explorer pages. Replay mode: the Skala energies were computed in advance.

Keys: Space plays or pauses, Left and Right step, Home restarts. One click takes 1.5 s at speed 1. Start 1 to 40 is one of 40 seeded first clicks, spaced evenly through the stored points.

Check failed: the live estimate differs from the recorded one, or a rule-based click matched no live suggestion.
